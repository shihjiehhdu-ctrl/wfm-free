現在我們來讀一段社會科的短文。先聽一次，不用停下來查字。

Fifty years ago, the air in our city was dirty. Factories produced smoke all day, and the pollution made many people ill.

The city government made a decision: it would reduce the number of old buses and make a large investment in clean transportation.

At first, many people disagreed. They thought the plan was impossible and the cost was unreasonable.

But the improvement was unexpected. Within ten years, the smoke had almost disappeared, and the city now exports electric buses to other countries.

Its story shows that the development of a city depends on the awareness of its people.

〔停頓 2 秒〕這段短文裡，畫底線的字都可以用你學過的零件拆開。我們一起看三個。

disagreed：dis 是「相反」，agree 是同意，所以是不同意。At first, many people disagreed. 一開始，很多人反對這個計畫。

unreasonable：最外面是 un，裡面是 reasonable，「合理的」。They thought the cost was unreasonable. 他們覺得花這麼多錢不合理。

awareness：aware 加 ness，把「注意到、知道」這種狀態打包成名詞。The development of a city depends on the awareness of its people. 城市能不能進步，要看大家有沒有意識到問題。

剩下的字，換你自己拆。點一下畫底線的字，它會被帶到「採集」，原句也會一起帶過去。
